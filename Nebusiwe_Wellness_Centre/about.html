About Nebusiwe Wellness Centre

Nebusiwe Wellness Centre is a professional primary healthcare and wellness clinic committed to providing accessible, affordable and quality healthcare to individuals, families and businesses.

We provide accessible, affordable and quality healthcare to individuals, families and businesses, and we promote healthy lifestyles in the communities we serve.

We aim to become one of the leading private wellness clinics in Springs and all surrounding communities by delivering excellent patient care, promoting healthy lifestyles and supporting employers with workplace health services.

Our four guiding values:

  1. Compassion: we care about the person, not only the condition.
  2. Professionalism: we keep high standards in everything we do.
  3. Affordability: quality healthcare should be within reach.
  4. Respect: every patient and family is treated with dignity.
